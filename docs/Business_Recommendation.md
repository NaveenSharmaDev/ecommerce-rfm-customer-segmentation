# Business Recommendation — RFM Customer Segmentation

## Executive conclusion
The upgraded analysis identifies five actionable customer segments using RFM scoring and K-Means. The best commercial strategy is to **protect Champions and win back At-Risk Customers**, while converting Potential Loyalists into repeat buyers.

## Current economics
- Customers: **4,334**
- Revenue: **£8,737,227.64**
- Champions: **312 (7.2%)** of customers; **51.5%** of revenue.
- Loyal Customers: **962 (22.2%)**; **24.5%** of revenue.
- At-Risk Customers: **1,027 (23.7%)**; **16.6%** of revenue.
- About **26.1%** of customers account for 80% of customer revenue.
- About **21.4%** of products account for 80% of product revenue.

## Segment playbook
### Champions — Protect
VIP loyalty, early access, premium bundles, referral rewards and personalized appreciation.

### Loyal Customers — Grow
Cross-sell complementary products, upsell premium variants and use loyalty-point campaigns.

### At-Risk Customers — Win Back
Trigger personalized win-back journeys, product recommendations and controlled limited-time offers.

### Potential Loyalists — Convert
Use second-purchase incentives, product discovery campaigns and post-purchase recommendations.

### Lost Customers — Reactivate Selectively
Run low-cost reactivation tests and suppress expensive incentives for persistent non-responders.

## KPIs to monitor
1. Segment size
2. Revenue by segment
3. Repeat purchase rate
4. Average order value
5. At-Risk reactivation rate
6. Revenue recovered from win-back campaigns
7. Champion retention rate

## Model note
K-Means uses log-transformed and standardized Recency, Frequency and Monetary values. Five clusters are used for business interpretability. Current silhouette score: **0.3172**.
